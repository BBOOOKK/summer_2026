B. Conversion
1. tf2::getYaw(quat_t);
2. Eigen::Quaterniond q(quat_msg.w, quat_msg.x, quat_msg.y, quat_msg.z);
   Eigen::Matrix3d R = q.normalized().toRotationMatrix();

The Eigen quaternion constructor uses (w, x, y, z). ROS message fields
are named x, y, z, w. R maps body-frame coordinates to the world frame.
The incoming quaternion must be nonzero before normalization.

Required headers:
#include <tf2_geometry_msgs/tf2_geometry_msgs.h>
#include <tf2/utils.h>
#include <Eigen/Geometry>
